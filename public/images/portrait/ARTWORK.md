# Portrait artwork

Edited with the built-in image_gen tool from the user-provided photograph. The PNG preserves transparency; the WebP is an optimized delivery copy with its alpha channel retained. The website adds perspective tilt and shadows using CSS.

## Final prompt

Use case: background-extraction. Edit target: the supplied photo of the man in a dark patterned kurta adjusting his wristwatch. Create a precise photographic cutout of ONLY this foreground man on a genuinely transparent alpha background. Remove all trees, sky, ground, people and objects behind him. Preserve his exact facial identity, facial structure, skin texture, hairstyle, beard, gaze, body proportions, pose, hands, watch, ring and clothing pattern. Do not redesign, beautify, cartoonize or turn him into a different person. Keep all hair and both elbows within frame, crop below his waist as in the original, center the cutout tightly with a small transparent margin above and at each side. Clean natural edges with no white fringe. Preserve natural dimensional photographic lighting; this asset will be displayed in a 3D-tilting website hero. No added text, objects, shadows on backdrop, frame, solid background, or checkerboard. Output transparent PNG.
